Questions
Does it work without signal?

Yes. All tables, calculators and 3D models are built into the app. Nothing needs the internet.

Where do the numbers come from?

ASME B36.10M, B36.19M, B16.5, B16.9, B16.10, B16.11, B16.20, B1.20.1 and MSS SP-43/95/97, with calculators following ASME B31.3 where noted. Olet and swage figures are typical manufacturer values. It's a field reference, so always check critical dimensions against the current standard and your project specification.

Inches or millimetres?

Both. Choose imperial (feet-inch-fractions, psi, °F) or metric (mm, bar, °C) and switch any time. Every value converts straight away.

Is there an iPhone version?

Pipewise is on Android today. If you'd like it on iPhone, email hello@sterin.dev. Demand decides what comes next.

What data does the app collect?

No accounts and no ads. Your settings and saved calculations stay on your phone. See the privacy policy for the full details.

Refunds?

Purchases are handled by Google Play, including refunds under Google Play's policy. If something isn't right, email me first and I'll do my best to fix it.
