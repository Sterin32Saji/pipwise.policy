# Pipewise — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2 November 2026
**App:** Pipewise (Android, package `com.pipewise.pipewise`)
**Developer:** Sterin Saji, United Kingdom
**Contact:** [hello@sterin.dev](mailto:hello@sterin.dev)

Pipewise is a paid piping reference and calculator app. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.
In short: **Pipewise has no accounts, no ads, and does not sell or share your personal information.**

---

## 1. Information stored on your device only

Pipewise keeps the following on your phone so it remembers how you work. None of it is sent to the developer.

- Your settings: unit system (imperial or metric), fraction rounding, theme, vibration, and 3D/drawing preference
- Your working pipe size, flange class and schedule
- Items you pin to the Home screen and your recently opened tables and calculators
- Calculations you choose to save, including the name you give them and the values you entered
- The last values typed into each calculator

This data stays in the app's private storage. You can remove it at any time by deleting saved items in the app,
clearing the app's storage in Android Settings, or uninstalling the app.

## 2. Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To understand which tables and calculators are useful and what to improve, Pipewise may use **Google Analytics for Firebase**.
When it is active, it records:

- Which screens, tables and calculators are opened
- Search words typed into the app's search box
- When a calculation is saved or shared (the calculator's name only, not your values)
- Standard device and app information collected by Firebase, such as device model, Android version, app version,
  approximate location (country or region, derived from your IP address) and an app-instance identifier

It does **not** record your name, email address, precise location, or the numbers you enter into calculators.
Analytics data is processed by Google under the
[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and
[Firebase data-processing terms](https://firebase.google.com/terms/data-processing-terms), and is kept for the retention period
set in Google Analytics (no longer than 14 months). The app does not use the data for advertising.

## 3. Purchases

Pipewise is bought through **Google Play**. Payment is handled entirely by Google; the developer never sees your card or
payment details. Google may share limited order information (such as an order number and country) with the developer for
accounting and refunds. See the [Google Play Terms of Service](https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/) and the
[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 4. When you contact the developer

The "Email" and "Report a problem" buttons open your own email app with a message already filled in. Depending on where you
press the button, the message may include the calculator or table name, the values you entered, the app version, your device
platform and your unit system. Nothing is sent until you choose to send it, and you can edit or delete anything first.

If you email [hello@sterin.dev](mailto:hello@sterin.dev), your email address and message are used only to reply to you and
to fix or improve the app. They are not shared, and are deleted when no longer needed.

## 5. Sharing a calculation

When you tap **Share**, Pipewise passes a text summary of the calculation to the app you pick (for example WhatsApp or email).
What happens next is controlled by that app.

## 6. Permissions

Pipewise requests only **Internet** access, which is used for the optional usage statistics above. It does not ask for your
location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

## 7. Advertising

Pipewise contains **no advertising** and no advertising SDKs.

## 8. Children

Pipewise is a professional tool for engineers, pipefitters and welders. It is not directed at children under 13 (16 in the
UK and EEA), and the developer does not knowingly collect information from children.

## 9. Your rights (UK GDPR, EU GDPR and similar laws)

You can ask to access, correct or delete personal information the developer holds about you, object to its use, or complain
to a data-protection authority. In the UK that is the
[Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/). Because Pipewise has no accounts, the developer
normally holds nothing about you except emails you have sent. To make a request, email
[hello@sterin.dev](mailto:hello@sterin.dev).

The legal basis for processing is the developer's legitimate interest in running and improving the app, and, for emails,
replying to you.

## 10. Security

On-device data is kept in the app's private storage, protected by Android. Data sent to Google is encrypted in transit.

## 11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new "Last updated" date. Significant changes will be
mentioned in the app's release notes.
